About us/

The Makers & Tech Showcase is run by volunteers from BCS Shropshire, the local branch of BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT.

BCS Shropshire

We bring together people across Shropshire and Telford who work with, study or just love technology. We run talks, round tables and fireside chats on topics from AI and quantum computing to cyber security.

Everyone is welcome, members and non-members alike, and most of our events are free.

BCS

BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT, is the UK's professional body for IT. Founded in 1957, it's a charity with a Royal Charter and over 70,000 members in 150 countries.

Its mission is to make sure everyone's experience with technology is positive, by raising standards across the profession.
