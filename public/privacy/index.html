Privacy notice

This notice covers the forms on this website: register interest, book a table, sponsor pack and get in touch.

Who we are

The Makers & Tech Showcase is run by volunteers from the Shropshire Branch of BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT. BCS is a registered charity (England & Wales 292786, Scotland SC051487), registered office 3 Newbridge Square, Swindon SN1 1BY. BCS's own privacy policy is at bcs.org.

What we collect and why

Only what you type into a form: usually your name, email address and your message, plus anything else the form asks for (such as the zone and power you need, or your company). We use it to reply to you and to organise the showcase. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

How it reaches us

When you press send, your details go through Web3Forms, a form service that turns them into an email to the organising team at jason.lewis@bcs-shropshire.org. We don't add you to any mailing list, sell your details or share them outside the organising team.

How long we keep it

Until the showcase is over and any follow-up is done, and no later than 31 December 2027, unless you ask us to keep in touch about future events.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, at any time. Email jason.lewis@bcs-shropshire.org. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The retro TV video only loads from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play.
